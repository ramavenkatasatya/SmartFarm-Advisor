Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using SmartFarm Advisor

General Information

What is SmartFarm Advisor? +
SmartFarm Advisor is an AI-powered platform designed to help farmers make data-driven decisions about crop selection, soil management, and weather planning.
Is the service free to use? +
Yes, SmartFarm Advisor is currently a completely free tool designed to support the agricultural community and promote sustainable, data-driven farming practices.
Is there a mobile app available for SmartFarm Advisor? +
SmartFarm Advisor is built as a fully responsive web application, which means it works perfectly on your mobile phone's web browser without needing to download a separate app from an app store.
What languages are supported? +
Currently, the platform supports English, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, and Malayalam through our integrated translation tool available in the navigation bar.

Features & Functionality

How does the Crop Advisor work? +
It uses a machine learning model to analyze your soil parameters (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium, pH) along with local weather data (temperature, humidity, rainfall) to recommend the most suitable crop for your farm.
How does the Profitability Analysis work? +
Our financial tools take your farm's size and custom overhead costs into account. We compare them against average yields and current market prices to estimate potential revenue, net profit, and overall cost breakdowns for each recommended crop.
What does the "EcoFit" score mean? +
The EcoFit score is an indicator of how well a particular crop naturally aligns with your local environment. A higher score means the crop is highly suited for your soil and weather, requiring fewer external interventions like excessive watering or fertilizers.
Can it help with irrigation planning? +
While our primary focus is currently on crop selection, profitability, and weather matching, we are actively developing dedicated Irrigation and Soil Intelligence tools that will be released very soon.
Can I track my past crop recommendations? +
Yes! Simply head to the "History" tab in the navigation menu. There, you can see all your previous soil parameters, weather inputs, and the crops that were recommended to you at that time.

Data & Privacy

Is my farm's data secure? +
Yes, all farm profiles and historical advisories are securely stored in a local database attached to your account, ensuring your farm data remains private and easily accessible whenever you need it.
Do I need internet access to use this? +
An internet connection is required to fetch real-time weather data and location information, but your core historical records and saved farm profiles can be viewed anytime.
How accurate is the weather data? +
We integrate with reliable third-party weather APIs (such as Open-Meteo) to provide highly accurate, hyper-local 7-day forecasts based precisely on your farm's GPS coordinates.

Farm Management

Can I manage multiple farms? +
Absolutely. The "My Farm" feature allows you to create and manage multiple profiles, each with its own unique soil composition, area size, and location parameters.
How do I update my farm's location? +
You can update your location by going to the "My Farm" tab, editing your farm profile, and clicking the "Detect Location" button, or by manually adjusting your latitude and longitude.
How do I get my soil N-P-K and pH values? +
You can obtain these values by sending a sample of your farm's soil to a local agricultural testing laboratory, or by using a reliable at-home digital soil testing kit.
